# Consumer LP UI benchmarks

1. Make one action dominant: amount in, index positions out, then wallet review.
2. Put real token, network, and venue marks on the product surface.
3. Show Fee APR, 24h volume, and TVL for each pool. Fee APR is the simple annualization of the trailing 24-hour fee pace, not APY.
4. Show Una’s fee and network costs in the review and receipt states.
5. Keep conversational agents out of navigation. Index curation, monitoring, and risk policy are internal machinery.
6. Do not offer automatic rebalancing without durable user authorization, an execution policy, and cost-aware safeguards.
